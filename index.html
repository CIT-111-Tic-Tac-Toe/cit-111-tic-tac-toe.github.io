Tic-Tac-Toe

A two-player game. Squares are numbered 1 through 9, left to right, top to bottom. Player X goes first, then players alternate. Use Tab or the Arrow keys to move between squares and press Enter or Space to place your mark, or press a number key 1 through 9 to select that square directly. The first player to line up three marks in a row, column, or diagonal wins.

Player X’s turn.
